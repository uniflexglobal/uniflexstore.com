The 90-second version — say this before you start clicking

Say this "Right now, when someone fills out our website's quote form, it's just an email — nothing tracked, nobody assigned. This system fixes that. A lead comes in, a Caller works it and qualifies it, an Admin — or a trusted senior Caller — hands it to a Dispatcher, the Dispatcher signs the carrier up, gets their trucks and paperwork on file, books loads for them, and we invoice our dispatch fee. Every step is logged. I'm going to show you the whole thing end to end, then show each of you your own screen."
Caller

Part 1 — Log in as Talha

localhost:3000/crm/login → caller@uniflexlogistics.com / Crm@12345

Dashboard

First thing after login. Two numbers: how many prospects are on the call list today, and how many have been qualified this week. This is a Caller's "what do I do today" screen — nothing more.

Call List (sidebar)

This is the actual job. Every row is someone to call.

  • Point out name, phone number, and the copy icon next to it — click it, show the toast, mention it copies as +1XXXXXXXXXX ready to paste into a dialer.
  • Point out the status filter tabs across the top (New, No answer, Call back later, etc.).
  1. Click "Call" on any row that isn't already Qualified. A dialog opens with four outcome buttons: No answer / Call back later / Not interested / Do not call — click one live, show the toast, show the row's status badge update.
  2. Re-open a fresh one and click the teal "This call was successful — Qualify" button instead.
  3. Fill the qualify form — name, phone, email, MC number, truck type, weight, preferred route, availability date, notes.
  4. Submit — and point out the "Assign to dispatcher now" dropdown, since Talha is a senior Caller. Leave it blank for this demo so it lands in Admin's queue instead — that's Part 2.

Import Prospects (sidebar)

Show the upload box — accepts CSV or Excel with at minimum a Name and Phone column. Upload a real sample list live if you have one; otherwise just explain the flow.

Leads (sidebar)

Talha's own history of everyone he's qualified, and what happened after — still waiting, assigned, or signed.

For Callers on your team: your whole day lives on the Call List page. Qualify a lead right and it's out of your hands — Admin or the Dispatcher takes it from here.
Admin

Part 2 — Log in as Ahmed

Sign out, log back in as admin@uniflexlogistics.com / admin12345

Dashboard

  • Company-wide numbers up top: prospects, leads, active carriers, staff, loads this month, revenue this month, documents expiring.
  • The big one: the "Awaiting Assignment" queue sits right on the dashboard. The lead Talha just qualified in Part 1 should be sitting here.
  1. Click "Assign" on it, pick Osaid from the dropdown, confirm.
Say this"This is the one moment Admin controls who gets which carrier."

Leads (sidebar)

Same page Callers see, but Admin sees everyone's leads, with Qualified-by and Dispatcher columns, and can assign or delete any lead from here too (trash icon, admin-only).

Carriers (sidebar)

Every signed carrier company-wide. Click into one — show the tabs (Profile / Trucks / Drivers / Documents) and mention Admin can reassign a carrier to a different Dispatcher from the list view.

Documents (sidebar)

This is a big one to sell. Company-wide list of every uploaded document, colour-coded: red = expired, amber = expiring within 30 days, green = valid.

Say this"This is how we never get caught with an expired insurance cert again."

Dispatch Board · Invoices · Commissions (sidebar)

  • Dispatch Board — company-wide Kanban of every load in motion, across every Dispatcher.
  • Invoices — every dispatch-fee invoice, its status.
  • Commissions — manual ledger of what each staff member has earned, paid or not.

Reports (sidebar)

Revenue over time chart, the Prospect → Lead → Carrier conversion funnel, loads per Dispatcher, leads qualified per Caller, commission summary table.

Say this"This is how we'll know who's performing."

Staff (sidebar)

Save this for last — it's the one your team needs to actually use after today. Show "Add Staff": name, email, temporary password, role (Caller/Dispatcher/Admin), and the "senior caller" toggle.

Say this"This is how we create everyone's real login once we're done testing."
For Admin: you're the only one who sees everything. Your two jobs day-to-day are the Awaiting Assignment queue and keeping an eye on Documents.

Part 3 — Log in as Osaid

Sign out, log back in as dispatcher@uniflexlogistics.com / Crm@12345

Dashboard

Newly assigned leads, active carriers, documents expiring — a Dispatcher's own numbers only.

Leads (sidebar)

The lead Ahmed assigned in Part 2 should be sitting here now, status "Assigned."

Carriers (sidebar)

  1. Click into the new carrier record — created automatically the moment it was assigned.
  2. Point out the onboarding checklist banner at the top — W-9, Certificate of Insurance, MC/DOT Authority Letter, Dispatch Agreement. "Mark carrier Active" is greyed out until all four are on file.
  3. Open the Documents tab, click Upload document, pick a type, upload any test file, set an expiry date — show it land in the list with a View button.
  4. Once all four types exist (even reusing one test file four times is fine for the demo), the "Mark carrier Active" button lights up — click it.

Trucks / Drivers tabs

Add a truck (unit number, equipment type), add a driver, assign the driver to the truck.

Dispatch Board (sidebar)

  1. Click "New Load" — pick the now-active carrier, its truck/driver auto-populate as options, fill in origin/destination/rate/dispatch fee %, create it.
  2. Show it land in the Booked column of the Kanban board.
  3. Click into it — show the check-call log (log one), show the rate confirmation / POD upload slots, then move it through Dispatched → In Transit → Delivered.
Say this"It won't let you mark Delivered until at least one check call is logged — keeps the record honest."

Invoices (sidebar)

  1. Click "New Invoice", pick that delivered load — the fee amount pre-fills from rate × dispatch fee %.
  2. Create it, show the status move Draft → Sent → Paid.
For Dispatchers: once a lead lands on your desk, you own it end to end — sign them up, get their paperwork, run their loads, get us paid.

Closing the demo

  • What's not built yet, on purposeLive load-board search (DAT/Truckstop), automatic call/SMS logging, e-signatures — all need paid third-party accounts we don't have yet. The system is built to slot those in later without a rebuild.
  • What happens after todayGo to Staff (as Ahmed) and create everyone's real account, then have them log in and change nothing else — the test accounts (Ahmed/Osaid/Talha) can be deactivated once real ones exist.
  • If anything looks offNote it and send it over — this is still early and easy to adjust.
